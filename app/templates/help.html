Notifications

Android

  1. Allow notifications when your browser asks.
  2. Open Settings in On Track and choose Enable on this device.
  3. Keep the notification preferences you want enabled.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open On Track in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen.
  2. Open the installed On Track app from the Home Screen.
  3. In Settings, choose Enable on this device and allow notifications.

Notifications are reminders. Always check your published Month and Day roster.

Report an error

If roster information or a page looks wrong, open Settings → Report an error. Include what you expected and what happened; On Track records only the safe page path with your report.
