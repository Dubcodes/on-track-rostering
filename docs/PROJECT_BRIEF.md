# Project brief

On Track Rostering is a specialised race-production roster whose operational model can be rich while its everyday interface remains simple. A normal urgent change should remain: open a Race Day, edit a person or time, preview, publish.

On Track—not Deputy, a calendar feed, or a racing website—is authoritative. A workday may be incomplete. Managers may always enter operational facts manually. Provider data will later assist planning without overwriting an explicit operational value.

The foundation prioritises correctness, server-side authorization, immutable publication history, fast employee reads, graceful external-service isolation, responsive Month/Day screens, direct Windows/Python development against PostgreSQL, and a low-complexity Compose deployment on the separate production server.

This is not generic HR software. It has no payroll, leave bureaucracy, single mandatory job title, or automatic break deduction.
